Every cat has a job.

ALBANYANG (알바냥, "Job Cat Universe") is an original short-form animated cat character series. Each episode follows a cat on its first day at a new part-time job: a cafe, a bakery, a convenience store, an aquarium and more.

About the ALBANYANG publishing app

The ALBANYANG publishing app is a private tool used by the ALBANYANG studio to upload its own original videos and images to the studio's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X and Reddit. Posts are prepared and reviewed by the studio, then published with the platforms' official APIs only after the account owner connects the account and approves each post.

How it works

  1. The account owner connects the studio's own account through the platform's official login (OAuth).
  2. The studio prepares a video or image and a caption, and reviews it in the app.
  3. The owner approves it, and the app sends it to the platform as a draft or as a post.
