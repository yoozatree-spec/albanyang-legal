ALBANYANG (알바냥) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

ALBANYANG ("we", "the app") is a small content studio that publishes its own original cat-character videos and images to its own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X and Reddit) through the official APIs of those platforms. The app is operated by a single individual operator for the operator's own accounts only. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When the operator connects an account, the app receives an access token from the platform and, where permitted, basic profile information of that same account (account name, ID) and the metadata of content the app itself publishes. We do not collect data about other users, followers or viewers.

How we use information

Access tokens and account details are used only to upload and schedule the operator's own posts and to read the status of those posts. We do not sell, rent, share or use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are deleted when the operator disconnects the account or revokes access in the platform's settings.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to your account, email the contact below, or remove the app from your account's connected apps/permissions settings on the relevant platform. We will delete the data within 30 days.

Terms of Service

The app is provided for the operator's private use to publish original content. Use of third-party platforms through the app is subject to each platform's own terms.
