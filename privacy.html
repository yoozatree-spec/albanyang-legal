Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

ALBANYANG ("we", "the app") is a small studio that publishes its own original cat-character videos and images to its own social media accounts through the official APIs of YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X and Reddit. The app is operated by one individual operator for the operator's own accounts only and is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When the operator connects an account, the app receives an access token from the platform and, where permitted, basic profile information of that same account (name, ID) and metadata of the content the app itself publishes. We do not collect data about other users, followers or viewers.

How we use information

Tokens and account details are used only to upload and schedule the operator's own posts and to read the status of those posts. We do not sell, rent, share, or use the data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and deleted when the operator disconnects the account or revokes access in the platform's settings.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Data deletion

To request deletion of data related to your account, email yoozatree@gmail.com or remove the app from your connected apps/permissions settings on the relevant platform. Data is deleted within 30 days.
